Sparkle — Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to sparkleapp.destek@gmail.com. We read every message.

Common questions

Is there a subscription?

No. Sparkle is a one-time purchase on the App Store. There are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

I changed watches or reinstalled the app.

Download Sparkle again from the App Store with the same Apple Account; it is free to re-download. Your data (history, settings) stays on the old watch unless it is restored from a backup.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Reset Data, or delete the app.

Reminders do not arrive.

Check Settings → Notifications on the watch, and that Focus modes or Do Not Disturb are not silencing Sparkle.

Sparkle is a personal tracking and timing tool. It does not provide medical advice. Consult your doctor for medical decisions.

Häufige Fragen

Gibt es ein Abo?

Nein. Sparkle ist ein Einmalkauf im App Store. Es gibt keine Abos und keine In-App-Käufe.
